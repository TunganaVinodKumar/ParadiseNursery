================================================================================
                    🌿 PARADISE NURSERY — USER & RUN GUIDE 🌿
================================================================================

A modern, responsive e-commerce web application for discovering, exploring,
and purchasing curated indoor houseplants. Built with React 19 and Vite.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SYSTEM PREREQUISITES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Node.js: Version 18.0.0 or newer (Run `node -v` to check)
- npm: Version 9.0.0 or newer (Run `npm -v` to check)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW TO INSTALL & RUN LOCALLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Open your terminal in the project directory:
   cd v:\Projects\ParadiseNursery-master

Step 2: Install dependencies (if running for the first time):
   npm install

Step 3: Start the local development server:
   npm run dev

Step 4: Open your browser and navigate to:
   http://localhost:5173/

   (If port 5173 is busy, Vite will automatically use http://localhost:5174/
    or display the exact URL in your terminal output)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PRODUCTION BUILD & PREVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

To create an optimized production bundle:
   npm run build

To preview the built production site locally:
   npm run preview

To run the linter and verify code cleanliness:
   npm run lint

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW TO USE THE WEB APPLICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🌿 1. EXPLORING HOUSEPLANTS
   - Go to the "Plants" tab (http://localhost:5173/#/products).
   - Use the live search bar to search by name (e.g., "Tulsi", "Lavender"),
     benefits (e.g., "sleep", "oxygen"), or botanical names.
   - Click category filter pills:
     * All
     * Aromatic Plants
     * Medicinal Plants
     * Air Purifying Plants
     * Low Maintenance
   - Use the secondary filters:
     * "🐾 Pet Friendly Only" checkbox to filter non-toxic plants.
     * "Care" dropdown (All Levels, Easy Care, Moderate Care).
     * "Sort" dropdown (Featured, Price: Low to High, Price: High to Low,
       Highest Rated, Alphabetical A-Z).

🌿 2. PLANT QUICK VIEW & DETAILS
   - Hover over any plant card and click "🔍 Quick View" (or click the card).
   - View high-resolution botanical photography, care matrix (Light, Water,
     Difficulty, Temperature), pet safety ratings, dimensions, and customer reviews.
   - Adjust quantities directly and click "Add to Cart".

🌿 3. WISHLIST / FAVORITES
   - Click the heart icon (🤍 / ❤️) on any plant card or modal to save it.
   - Click the heart button in the top navbar to open your Wishlist Drawer.
   - Easily move plants from your wishlist directly into your cart.

🌿 4. INTERACTIVE PLANT MATCHER QUIZ
   - Click "✨ Plant Matcher" in the navbar or on the landing page banner.
   - Answer 3 quick lifestyle questions:
     1. Room sunlight conditions (Direct Sun, Indirect, Low Light).
     2. Pet safety requirements (Cats/dogs at home).
     3. Watering routine (Low-maintenance vs. weekly care).
   - Get instant personalized plant recommendations with 1-click Add to Cart!

🌿 5. PLANT DOCTOR & CARE GUIDE
   - Click "📖 Care Guide" in the top navbar or footer.
   - Read essential guidelines on the soil finger test, sunlight definitions,
     drainage & repotting intervals, and non-toxic pet varieties.

🌿 6. CART, COUPONS & MULTI-STEP CHECKOUT
   - Click "🛒 Cart" in the navbar to open your cart.
   - Track your progress on the "Free Delivery Meter" (Unlocks at ₹ 25.00).
   - Apply discount codes in the promo box:
     * WELCOME10  -> 10% off your entire order
     * GREEN20    -> ₹ 5.00 flat discount
     * FREESHIP   -> Free delivery on any order
   - Click "Proceed to Checkout":
     * Step 1: Enter your delivery address, phone, email, and preferred time slot.
     * Step 2: Choose your payment method (Instant UPI, Credit/Debit Card, or COD).
     * Step 3: Watch your order process securely.
     * Step 4: View and print your itemized receipt with unique Order ID (PN-XXXXXX).

🌿 7. DARK / LIGHT MODE
   - Click the sun/moon button (☀️ / 🌙) in the top navbar.
   - Smoothly switches the color palette, and your preference is saved
     automatically for future visits.
